# Third-party components

Built into the cores:

| Component | Use | License |
|---|---|---|
| [ZX-Spectrum_MISTer](https://github.com/MiSTer-devel/ZX-Spectrum_MISTer), pinned at `8502d492` | The MiSTer framework (`sys/`) of the eight Z80 cores, their tape players (`tape.sv`, `pzxplayer.sv`, `tzxplayer.vhd`) and the Spectrum +3 machine the CIP-04 profile is made from; a submodule (`src/core-{hc,cip,cubz}/vendor/spectrum`) | GPL-2.0-or-later and GPL-3.0-or-later, as its source headers state |
| T80, by Daniel Wallner, MikeJ, TobiFlex and Sorgelig (from the same repository) | The Z80 processor of the eight Z80 cores | Its own terms (notice below) |
| YM2149 / AY-3-8910, by MikeJ and Sorgelig | The AY sound chip of the HC and TIM-S cores (`hc_ay.sv`, `rp_ay.sv`, with a lookup table rewritten as a function) and of the CIP-04 | Its own terms (notice below) |
| u765, by Gyorgy Szombathelyi | The 8272 floppy controller of the HC, TIM-S, CoBra and CUB Z cores (`hc88_u765.sv`, `rp_u765.sv`, `cubz_u765.sv`, adapted) and of the CIP-04 | GPL-2.0-or-later |
| SDRAM controller, by Sorgelig | The memory of the TIM-S core (`rp_sdram.sv`) | GPL-3.0-or-later |
| [PCXT_MiSTer](https://github.com/MiSTer-devel/PCXT_MiSTer), pinned at `fa80b405` | The JUNIOR-XT core is built on it: the MiSTer framework, the PC/XT chipset and peripherals; a submodule (`src/core-juniorxt/vendor/pcxt`) | GPL-3.0 |
| MCL86, by Ted Fried and Marcus Andrade (from PCXT_MiSTer) | The 8086/8088 processor of the JUNIOR-XT core | MIT (notice below) |
| Floppy controller and FIFOs, by Aleksander Osman and Alexey Melnikov (from PCXT_MiSTer) | The diskette interface of the JUNIOR-XT core | BSD 2-Clause (notice below) |
| Altera PLL and platform files | Generated by Quartus for the DE10-Nano | Intel's terms for use with its FPGA devices |

Shipped with the cores (`games/<CORE>/`) and in the assets archive, not part of the source:

| Data | Use | License |
|---|---|---|
| The machines' ROM images | The firmware of each computer, preserved by the [z80-romania.ro](https://www.z80-romania.ro/) archive and the sources named in each core's `provenance.md` and `sources.lock.json`; the CoBra's also from the [CoBra Project](https://cobrasov.com/CoBra%20Project/) | Their makers'; original firmware keeps its owners' rights |
| Spectrum +3 system ROMs (TIM-S Plus, CIP-04) | The system the machines ran | Amstrad's (notice below) |
| The manufacturers' demo cassettes and system disks | The demos of the HC and CIP, the CP/M disks of the HC-91, HC-2000, CoBra and CUB Z | Their makers' |
| CP/M 2.2 (HC-88 system disk) | CCP and BDOS built from the original source, with a BIOS of the project's own | The licence in [hc/licenses/cpm22.txt](hc/licenses/cpm22.txt) |
| [FreeDOS PC/XT boot floppy](https://jorisvr.nl/article/freedos), by Joris van Rantwijk (JUNIOR-XT demo disk) | FreeDOS kernel 2036, FreeCOM 0.82 pl 3, FORMAT and CHKDSK | GPL-2.0-or-later; their sources are in the assets archive (`docs/juniorxt/reference/`), from the addresses in [juniorxt/software-sources.lock.json](juniorxt/software-sources.lock.json) |

Each core's own notes are in its `licenses/README.md` or `licenses.md` and its `provenance.md`.
